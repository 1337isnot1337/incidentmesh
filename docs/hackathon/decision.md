# Concept decision — 2026-09-05

Three candidates were compared before locking the build:

| Candidate | Brief fit | Genuine concurrency | Mozaik fit | Demo clarity | Feasibility | Decision |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| IncidentMesh incident room | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | **pick** |
| Parallel hypothesis testing | 5 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 5 | runner-up |
| Self-reviewing codebase | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | defer |

IncidentMesh won because the event sequence is immediately inspectable, independent signals are naturally simultaneous, and aggregate evidence creates a visible adaptive decision. The earlier research-swarm scaffold contributed only deterministic runtime/test discipline; the product uses the current Mozaik 4.x API.

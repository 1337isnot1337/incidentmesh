# Concept decision — 2026-09-05

Three serious candidates were compared before locking the build:

| Candidate | Brief fit | Genuine concurrency | Mozaik fit | Demo clarity | Feasibility | Decision |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| IncidentMesh live incident room | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | **pick** |
| Parallel hypothesis testing | 5 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 5 | runner-up |
| Self-reviewing codebase | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | defer |

IncidentMesh won because the event sequence is immediately understandable, independent signals are naturally simultaneous, and a shared confidence/risk state creates a visible adaptive turn. The prior research-swarm scaffold was reused only for its deterministic runtime/test discipline; its 3.x architecture was replaced with the current 4.x runtime.

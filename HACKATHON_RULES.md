# JigJoy × daily.dev × Hyperskill Hackathon — verified rules

Verified 2026-09-05 from the official pages:

- Official event: <https://build.jigjoy.ai/>
- Official rules: <https://build.jigjoy.ai/rules>
- Official submission page: <https://build.jigjoy.ai/submit>
- Build window: September 5–6, 2026; worldwide and online.
- Deadline: Monday, September 7, 2026 at 09:00 CET / 03:00 ET / midnight Sunday PT.
- Updates: resubmission is allowed until the deadline; the latest entry is judged.
- Entry: solo or a team of up to four; one entry per team and one team per person.
- Eligibility: worldwide; at least 18 years old or the higher local age of majority.
- Project rule: the substance must be built during the Hackathon; existing frameworks, libraries, and boilerplate are allowed.
- Required technology: the project must use `@mozaik-ai/core` (Mozaik) and run at least two concurrent agents.
- Required submission fields: project name, accessible repository, description, and an explanation of which agents run concurrently, what state they share, and how they coordinate.
- Submission identity: the submission page asks for the address used during application; an optional team name may be left blank for a solo entry. Screenshots can be uploaded; deployment and demo/video URLs are optional fields.
- Strongly recommended: demo/video, live deployment, and screenshots. A submission judges cannot open, run, or verify may be excluded.
- Technical brief: build a working system where several agents run at the same time, share state, and coordinate/react rather than follow a fixed sequence. Mozaik is the open-source TypeScript runtime.
- Judging emphasis: genuine concurrency first, alongside technical execution, creativity, and clarity of the demo.
- Cash prizes: 1st $500, 2nd $300, 3rd $200. Cash is distributed through Upwork.
- Winners: announced September 13, 2026; judging may take up to seven days, with a possible seven-day extension.

The official public rules do not require a particular product domain, deployment platform, or model provider. The project therefore keeps a deterministic provider-free demo and an optional real-model path.
